Catalogue

Catalogue scope

This Python app persists data in data/numismatics.sqlite. The built-in catalogue covers U.S. type coins plus major modern U.S. Mint circulating and collectible design programs: State/DC/Territory quarters, America the Beautiful quarters, American Women quarters, 2026 Semiquincentennial quarters, Presidential dollars, Native American dollars, and American Innovation dollars through 2026. It also imports the Wikipedia decade tables for U.S. commemorative coins and medals from pre-1900 through the 2020s.

Additional market coins can be added from the app or imported from CSV with python3 app.py --import-catalog-csv path/to/catalog.csv. CSV columns: id,name,denomination,years,program,category,description,source,tags.
